# Can two annual enterprise surveys support the same AI adoption claim?

Eurostat added a media-generation item in 2025 and expanded language generation to mention programming code. Define the comparable basket as the six other technology categories. Compare 2024 with 2025 for the EU27 aggregate and the three enterprise size classes. Retain the official all-enterprise share supplied by Eurostat.

Report each year's headline, each item, and the minimum and maximum possible share using at least one of the six categories. Enumerate all binary patterns for that year's questionnaire. Constrain their population shares by the published item, any-use, two-or-more and three-or-more percentages. Allow half a hundredth of a percentage point for rounding. Run the same analysis without the two multi-use totals to measure what those totals add.

Calculate change bounds across the two independent survey distributions. Return a directional conclusion only if the whole interval is on one side of zero. These are identification bounds conditional on reported survey estimates. They exclude sampling uncertainty. Provide feasible allocations and objective bounds for independent verification.

A research lead can use this to decide which trends to publish and which cross-tabulation to request. The public headline trends and the questionnaire amendments were visible when this design was written.
